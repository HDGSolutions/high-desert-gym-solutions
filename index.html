Meet the Owner

High Desert Gym Solutions is led by Nate Rubio, a fitness equipment specialist serving commercial and residential clients throughout New Mexico.

With hands-on experience repairing, maintaining, installing, and relocating fitness equipment from many of the industry's leading manufacturers, Nate understands the importance of minimizing downtime and keeping facilities operating safely and efficiently.

From commercial fitness centers and apartment communities to hotels, schools, corporate wellness centers, and home gyms, High Desert Gym Solutions delivers dependable service, responsive communication, and professional results.
